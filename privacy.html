Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 · Applies to the Oto app and otoshiyori.health

The short version

Oto is built on one rule: she initiates, family receives. The app does not track its user. There is no GPS or location collection, no activity monitoring, no camera or microphone surveillance, and no advertising. Everything a family member sees is something the user chose to send.

What Oto stores

  • Her contacts (names, photos, phone numbers), her schedule, and her preferences — stored on the device.
  • Check-ins ("I'm okay today") and help requests — delivered to the family members she or her family added during setup, then kept only as recent history.

What Oto never collects

  • Location, movement, or GPS data of any kind.
  • Ambient audio, video, photos, or screen activity.
  • Usage analytics tied to her identity, advertising identifiers, or any data sold or shared with third parties.

Calls

Calls placed from Oto go through the phone's own dialer or calling apps. Oto does not record, route, or listen to calls.

This website

otoshiyori.health uses privacy-friendly, cookieless page counting (GoatCounter) to see how many people visit. No personal profiles, no cross-site tracking, no ads.

Changes and contact

Oto is in active development; this policy will be updated as the app takes final shape, and material changes will be noted here. Questions: support@ubersoft.pro.
