Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026 · Applies to the Oto app and otoshiyori.health

Oto (the "app") is built and published by Ubersoft ("we," "us," "our") on one rule: they initiate, family receives. The app does not track its user — no location, no monitoring, no surveillance. Everything a family member sees is something they chose to send.

In plain terms
  • No tracking. No GPS, no location, no movement data, no activity monitoring.
  • No surveillance. No ambient audio, video, photos, or screen activity — ever.
  • They choose what's shared. Check-ins and help requests go only to the family they approved.
  • No ads, nothing sold. No advertising identifiers, no analytics tied to them, no data sold or shared.

01 What Oto stores on the device

  • Their contacts (names, photos, phone numbers), their schedule, and their preferences — stored on the device.

02 Check-ins and help requests — the one thing that leaves

Check-ins ("I'm okay today") and help requests are the only information that leaves the device. They are delivered only to the family members added during setup, and are kept only as recent history. Nothing is sent that the user didn't choose to send.

03 What Oto never collects

  • Location, movement, or GPS data of any kind.
  • Ambient audio, video, photos, or screen activity.
  • Usage analytics tied to their identity, advertising identifiers, or any data sold or shared with third parties.

04 Calls

Calls placed from Oto go through the phone's own dialer or calling apps. Oto does not record, route, or listen to calls.

05 This website

otoshiyori.health uses privacy-friendly, cookieless page counting (GoatCounter) to see how many people visit. No personal profiles, no cross-site tracking, no ads.

06 Changes to this policy

Oto is in active development; this policy will be updated as the app takes final shape, and material changes will be noted here with a new effective date.

07 Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@ubersoft.pro.
